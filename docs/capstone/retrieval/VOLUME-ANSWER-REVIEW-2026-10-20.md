## What is the average pancreas volume reported in research?

One MRI study reported a mean whole-pancreas volume of 82.6 cm³ in 14 people with normal glucose tolerance in the comparison group. The reported ±4.8 cm³ is the standard error of the mean (SEM): uncertainty in the estimated group mean, rather than a range of individual sizes. The study groups were matched for age, weight and sex.

Treat this as a result from a selected study group, not a universal average or a CT reference range. It cannot determine whether an individual scan is normal or abnormal.

Source: [Macauley et al., 2015](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4423920/).
Evidence: abstract methods (groups, MRI, matching), image-analysis method (whole-organ cm³), statistics (mean±SEM), and results (group-specific values).
