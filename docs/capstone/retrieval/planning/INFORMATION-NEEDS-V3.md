# Information needs v3 — general pancreatic disease context

**State:** Engineering reconciliation of Quinton's October 17 disease-expert direction; development
labels and final evaluation freeze remain pending human review.

V3 inherits all v1 needs and RF1–RF9 boundaries through [v2](INFORMATION-NEEDS-V2.md), and adds:

| ID | Need | Answer boundary |
|---|---|---|
| N1.6 | What general pancreatic disease associations and imaging features help explain pancreatic enlargement or distinguish inflammatory appearances from tumors? | Describe the cited disease subtype, modality, contrast phase and overlap. No size-to-diagnosis rule, individualized probability, current-scan diagnosis, treatment advice or claim that an outline reveals these imaging findings. |

The five families remain unchanged; the count becomes 26 information needs. The evaluation remains
30 development plus 30 protected questions, each split with 20 answerable and 10 unsupported/refused.
N1.4 continues to cover limitations of indirect signs for delineation; N1.6 does not turn those signs
into an evidenced boundary or patient diagnostic checklist. Quantified scan findings and reference
comparisons remain the separate required measurement extension after core acceptance.

Authority is the user's explicit direction to use pancreatitis/cancer literature and questions about
head-size associations and additional distinguishing findings. This document formalizes that accepted
product scope; it does not claim that the user has approved new labels or changed clinical boundaries.

This reconciliation follows the exposed ten-case v6 quality diagnostic. The two disease wordings
predate that run and were already stakeholder/development examples. This is not a blind preregistration,
and the diagnostic's misses or model failures are not held-out evidence. No new retrieval or prompt
comparison has been performed to choose these needs. Prior v1/v2 requirements stay unchanged.

The development v3 schema binds this document, its v2 parent and the v1 base separately. The private
v1 schema is not extended by this amendment; its operator, role and scope admission require a separate
version before protected authoring can proceed.
