## Trello and hours protocol

Canonical board:
https://trello.com/b/vac4k8Po/prowl-quinton-evans-capstone

Quinton approved the instructor's board structure on October 6: Resources, Backlog, To-Do,
In-Progress, Done and weekly completion lists. Resources holds reusable references. Backlog holds
future milestones; To-Do holds selected current-week tasks awaiting work, input or a dependency.
Only actively underway work belongs in In-Progress. Inactive blocked work stays in To-Do with
a named Blocked label and its dependency/restart condition; N4 stays with its retrieval owner.
Keep one card per bounded task, with owner, scope, finish line, evidence, next action and a course
deadline or labeled internal target. At a meaningful handback or changed blocker, update the
existing card and verify readback. Mark Done only when its finish line is met. Prepared course
drafts awaiting submission/presentation confirmation stay in To-Do when nobody is actively
working on them.

Done holds completed tasks from the current week. At weekly close, move those cards into Week N
Complete, preserving their contents and history. Week 1 Complete stays empty until that close.
Pre-course Complete preserves the four earlier PRE cards separately from the Week 1 total.
Do not delete, archive or replace existing cards to reorganize the board. The October 6 cleanup
preserves existing card content; any later content reconciliation is a separate task.

Update [the hours log](HOURS-LOG.md) at each handback when Quinton supplies active time. Keep
unconfirmed estimates visible and separate. Do not infer time from chat gaps, unattended jobs or
several agents' elapsed time. Review the total once per week; this is a work-session practice,
not a newly scheduled automation.
